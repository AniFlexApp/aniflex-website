序章 — PROLOGUE

You wake on the road, robbed of everything.

A traveler named Kiriyu finds you unconscious where the bandits left you. Before you can catch your breath, smoke rises over the village of Amahara — and the road pulls you straight into the fight.

On your final rep, something answers from inside you. The villagers have a name for it. The Masters have spent their lives learning to control it.

They call it Echo — a force every human carries, drawn out by physical effort. The body is the medium. Training is how it wakes.

響 — THE POWER WITHIN ALL

Your power is built out of who you actually are.

AniFlex turns your real workouts into an anime campaign. Physical effort draws out Echo — the power everyone in this world carries — and there are no classes to pick from. It takes its shape from four things: what you believe, what you want, what you survived and what you have actually done. Move the light to see how the mix changes the power.
